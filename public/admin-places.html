First-time setup & security

Enable Google sign-in in Firebase Authentication, authorize your hosting domain, and deploy the supplied Firestore rules and indexes. Full steps are in the Burmese user/admin guide included with the project.

Protect each Google account with a passkey or 2-Step Verification. Email addresses alone do not grant access. Sign-in stays in this browser tab when switching Events and Community profiles or reloading. Closing the tab ends the session. Idle sessions sign out after 15 minutes.

This editor manages new community profiles. The original directory snapshot, site design and donation links remain in the project files.
